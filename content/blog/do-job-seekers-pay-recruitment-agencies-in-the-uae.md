No. In the UAE, job seekers should not pay recruitment agencies. Recruitment costs are paid by the employer, and an agency that asks a candidate for money is a warning sign.

## What the law says

The UAE labour law, [Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021](https://u.ae/-/media/Mohre-2024/Federal-DecreeLaw-No-33-of-2021-Regarding-the-Regulation-of-Employment-Relationship-and-its-amendmen.pdf), states that employers may not charge workers recruitment or employment fees, whether directly or indirectly. The cost of hiring sits with the employer.

This article is general information, not legal advice. For questions about a specific situation, contact the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) or a qualified legal adviser.

## Common warning signs

Be careful if anyone offering you a job in the UAE:

- asks for a "registration", "processing" or "visa" fee before you start
- asks you to pay for a job offer or an interview
- contacts you from a personal email address and cannot show a company licence
- offers a salary that seems far too high for the role
- pressures you to pay or decide quickly

## How to check a job offer

- Ask for the full company name and licence details, and check the company exists.
- Do not pay money to receive a job offer.
- Check official guidance from MOHRE and the UAE government portal [u.ae](https://u.ae).
