The best recruitment agency in Dubai for your business is one that is properly licensed, understands your sector, screens every candidate before you meet them, and puts its fees and guarantees in writing. Before you sign with any agency, ask these seven questions.

## 1. Is the agency licensed for recruitment?

Recruitment in the UAE is a licensed activity. Ask which authority issued the agency's licence and which activities it covers, and check that recruitment is one of them. A licensed agency will share its licence details without hesitation.

## 2. Does it know your sector?

Hiring a site engineer is very different from hiring a leasing consultant or a hotel supervisor. Ask which roles and sectors the agency recruits for most often, and how it finds candidates for them. Sector knowledge means better short lists and fewer wasted interviews.

## 3. How does it screen candidates?

A good agency does more than forward CVs. Ask what happens before a candidate reaches you:

- Is every candidate interviewed?
- Are work history and experience checked?
- How many candidates will you see for each role?

You want a short list of people who fit, not a pile of CVs to sort through yourself.

## 4. Does it charge candidates?

It should not. Under UAE labour law, recruitment costs are paid by the employer, not the worker. An agency that charges job seekers is a red flag. We explain this in more detail in [Do job seekers pay recruitment agencies in the UAE?](/blog/do-job-seekers-pay-recruitment-agencies-in-the-uae)

## 5. What are the fees, and when are they paid?

Fee models differ between agencies. Ask for the fee structure in writing, including when payment is due and what it covers, so there are no surprises when you make a hire.

## 6. What happens if the hire does not work out?

Ask whether the agency offers a replacement or refund if a new hire leaves within an agreed period, and get the terms in writing.

## 7. How will it keep you updated?

Clear communication saves time. Agree how often you will get updates, who your contact person is, and the expected timeline from brief to short list.

## Summary

Licence, sector knowledge, real screening, no fees for candidates, clear pricing, a written guarantee and good communication: an agency that ticks all seven boxes is worth working with.
