An HR consultancy helps a company put the people side of the business in order: clear HR policies, an employee handbook, a sensible team structure, and someone to ask when HR questions come up. For growing companies in Dubai and across the UAE, it is often the fastest way to get HR right without hiring a full HR team.

## What an HR consultancy usually covers

- **HR policies:** leave, working hours, conduct, performance and other everyday rules, written for how your company actually works.
- **Employee handbook:** one document that explains rules, benefits and expectations to every team member.
- **Team structure:** roles, reporting lines and job descriptions that support the next stage of growth.
- **Hiring support:** clear role profiles and a consistent process for bringing in new people.
- **Ongoing advice:** practical guidance when day-to-day people questions arise.

Questions about employment law should still go to a qualified UAE legal adviser. A good HR consultancy works alongside them, not instead of them.

## Signs your company needs HR support

- You are hiring faster than you can write job descriptions and onboarding plans.
- Team members ask the same policy questions again and again.
- Rules are applied differently from one manager to the next.
- You have no single document that explains how the company works.

## HR consultancy or an in-house HR team?

An in-house HR team makes sense once a company is large enough to keep HR professionals fully busy. Before that point, an HR consultancy gives you experienced support when you need it, and can help you set up the foundations an in-house team will later build on.
