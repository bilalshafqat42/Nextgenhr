Outsourced HR gives a small or growing UAE company experienced HR support without hiring a full-time HR manager. An external HR team writes your contracts and policies, sets up appraisals and salary reviews, advises managers on day-to-day people questions and supports your hiring, either on an ongoing basis or for a defined project.

For many SMEs it is the practical middle ground: HR done properly, at a cost that fits the size of the business.

## What outsourced HR covers

Every provider scopes its service differently, but a good outsourced HR arrangement for a UAE company usually includes:

- **Employment documents:** offer letters, employment contracts and job descriptions, written for your company and reviewed against your own legal advice
- **Policies and an employee handbook:** working hours, leave, conduct, performance and other policies, so every employee knows the rules
- **Day-to-day HR advice:** a team your managers can call when a performance, attendance or conduct issue comes up
- **Performance and pay:** appraisal cycles, objectives, salary benchmarking and clear pay bands
- **Team structure:** an organisation chart, job grades and reporting lines that make sense as you grow
- **Hiring support:** interview support for your own hiring, or a full search for senior roles
- **Onboarding and exits:** a consistent process for new joiners and for employees who leave

## When outsourced HR makes sense

Outsourced HR is usually a good fit when:

- **You have no HR manager yet,** and the founder or finance team handles HR questions on top of their own work
- **Your team is growing quickly** and you need contracts, policies and structure before problems appear
- **You have an HR administrator** for payroll and paperwork, but no one senior to set policy and advise managers
- **You need a specific project done,** such as a handbook, a salary review or your first appraisal cycle
- **Your HR team is stretched** during a busy period, an expansion or a restructure

It is less suitable when you need someone on site every day to manage a large workforce. At that point, a full-time HR manager, supported by an external team for specialist projects, is often the better choice.

## Outsourced HR or a full-time HR manager?

| | Outsourced HR | Full-time HR manager |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | A fee agreed for the scope of work | A full salary and benefits package |
| Experience | A team with experience across many companies | One person's experience |
| Availability | Agreed contact times and response times | On site every working day |
| Flexibility | Scale up or down, or use for a single project | A fixed cost |
| Best for | SMEs, start-ups and companies with a small HR team | Larger companies with daily HR workload |

Many companies start with outsourced HR and hire an HR manager later. A good provider makes that step easier, because your contracts, policies and processes are already in place.

## What to look for in a provider

- **A licence for HR consultancy** from a UAE authority or free zone
- **Real UAE experience,** so advice reflects how hiring and HR work in the UAE
- **A written scope and fee,** agreed before any work starts, so there are no surprises
- **Clear contact arrangements:** who you speak to, how often and how quickly they reply
- **Honesty about legal questions:** a good HR provider works alongside your legal adviser on employment law questions rather than replacing them

## A note on UAE employment rules

Private-sector employment in mainland UAE is governed by the UAE labour law and MOHRE regulations, while some free zones, such as DIFC and ADGM, have their own employment laws. Your contracts and policies need to match the rules that apply to your company. An outsourced HR team helps you put the right documents and processes in place, and your legal adviser confirms the legal position.
